## This is not legal advice

Say it plainly, because it matters more than anything else on this page.

This site is **free public information**. It is not legal advice, immigration
advice, financial advice or careers advice. Reading it, or writing to us, does
not create a client relationship, an advisory relationship, a contract, or any
duty of care towards you. We are not lawyers, not an agency, not a consultancy
and not an intermediary, and we are paid nothing by anyone for what is written
here.

German law recognises this distinction directly. Under **§ 675 Abs. 2 BGB**, a
person who gives advice or a recommendation is not liable for damage arising
from following it, unless liability arises from a contract, from tort, or from
some other statutory provision. There is no contract between us and you, and
none is offered.

**What that means in practice.** Immigration law changes. Authorities in
different German cities apply the same rule differently, and are entitled to.
An answer that is correct in Frankfurt may not be correct in Rostock, and an
answer that was correct in August 2026 may not be correct when you read it.

So: every claim here carries the source it came from and the date it was
checked. **Check it against that source before you act on it.** For anything
that would be expensive or irreversible to get wrong — a marriage date, a
resignation, a loan, a flight — take advice from someone qualified who knows
your actual circumstances.

written down rather than quietly deleted.

If something here is wrong, out of date, or has cost you money because you
relied on it, we want to know. Write to
**[info@ausbildungfacts.org](mailto:info@ausbildungfacts.org)**.

You do not need to be polite about it, and you do not need to prove anything. If

told you otherwise — we will check it and either correct the page or explain why
